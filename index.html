Bottle Drop: Color Puzzle

A calm colour-sorting puzzle for Android. Tap a tube, tap another, and pour matching liquid until every tube holds a single colour.

90 levels, a new daily challenge, an endless mode and a free daily gift. Hints and undo are always free. Ads are optional, and you can switch them off in Settings.
