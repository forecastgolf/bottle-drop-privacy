Bottle Drop: Privacy Policy

Operator: Saltpine Studio
Contact: slicecityg@gmail.com
Last updated: 21 October 2026
Applies to: the Bottle Drop app for Android (the iOS version is not released and shows no ads)

In short

  • Bottle Drop has no account, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data ourselves.
  • Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Android version can show optional ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google collects some data to deliver them. Details are below.
  • The game is not designed for children.

1. What the game stores on your device

The game saves a small file on your device with: the levels you have finished, stars, coins, items you have bought with coins, goals you have claimed, your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, colour symbols, light theme, optional ads), your day streak, your best endless stage, and the date of the last daily challenge.

This file never leaves your device through the game. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The Android backup of this file is switched off, so reinstalling the app starts a new game.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The game itself has no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no server of ours. We do not receive any data from your device.

3. Optional ads (Google AdMob)

The Android version can offer an optional rewarded ad: you may watch a short ad to receive bonus coins. Nothing in the game requires it, and you can switch ads off in Settings under "Optional ads". Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, and to measure and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may process, for example:

  • your advertising ID and similar device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • how you interact with ads.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data and handles it under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data and do not combine it with anything.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads that use personal data are requested, using Google's consent tool. You can review or change your choice at any time: Settings, "Ad privacy options" (shown where it applies). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

If you do not consent, or if you switch ads off, you can still play the whole game.

4. Children

Bottle Drop is for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us at the address above.

5. Permissions on Android

  • Internet: used only by the ad SDK to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • The ad SDK may add network-state and advertising-ID permissions. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not share personal data because we do not have any. Data processed by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, you can use Google's privacy tools (https://myaccount.google.com) and the in-app ad privacy options. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us at the address above if you have any question about this policy.

8. Security

The save file is a plain file in the app's private storage. We do not transmit it. Ads are loaded over secure connections by the Google SDK.

9. Changes

If we add features that handle data (for example a different ad provider, analytics, online features or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
